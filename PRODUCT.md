# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and directors of tailor-made travel agencies and DMCs - typically the same person. Agencies running tailor-made trips (luxury, DMC, specialist receptives). Priority market: Spain (Barcelona, Madrid, Seville, Valencia). Also relevant for French-speaking and English-speaking tailor-made agencies and DMCs. They are not sysadmins - they know their business deeply, not software. They are currently running their operations on Excel, Word, email and WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Traviosoft is a complete management system for tailor-made travel agencies and DMCs, on the same level as the established travel software (Tourplan, Lemax, Kaptio, Ezus, Tourwriter). It covers the full client file lifecycle: client request, quote (PDF), order, supplier coordination, tracked payments, invoicing. The website lets visitors go as deep as they want: a clear home page, then one page per job (Sell, Book and run, Get paid, Your setup), pricing shown openly, an example trip anyone can open, and a Resources layer (comparisons, guides, glossary, Spain-only tax guides). Demo only, no self-serve signup. One quiet "Book a demo" link in the header; page bodies never push the demo.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clean, human. Direct and concrete - no marketing fluff, no SaaS buzzwords like "revolutionary" or "disruptive". Empathetic toward the daily reality of running a tailor-made agency or DMC. Confident and established, in the same register as the established vendors, without enterprise buzzwords. Support by email in ES/EN/FR from the people who build the product; data hosted in the EU. Built from real-world use; no invented client logos, numbers or reviews. Tone: like a knowledgeable peer, not a salesperson.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero-metric grids (big numbers, gradient accents)
- Sites that claim "100+ happy clients" or show fake testimonials
- Overly polished enterprise software marketing (Salesforce, HubSpot aesthetic)
- Any design that feels disconnected from the travel and hospitality world
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, side-stripe borders

## Design Principles

1. **Substance over decoration** - every section earns its place by answering a real question the user has while reading the page
2. **Honest by default** - never imply scale, testimonials or features that don't exist; the product's credibility comes from specificity, not from inflated claims
3. **The visitor decides** - one quiet demo link in the header, no repeated CTAs; prices are public; country-specific rules (REAV, Veri*Factu) are settings, never headline features
4. **Built for the expert** - assume the reader knows their business better than we do; address pain points precisely rather than explaining what a tailor-made agency is
5. **Multilingual by nature** - FR/EN/ES are equal citizens, not afterthoughts; the language switcher is visible on every page

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced motion support required (agencies are often running on older hardware or in high-contrast environments). All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Language switcher must be clearly labeled and functional without JavaScript for screen readers.
